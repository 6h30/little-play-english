# Little Play English (trial)

English for children aged 1 to 5, as short games: first words, colours, short sentences, syllables, rhymes, letters and tiny stories.
A daily adventure of about 5 minutes ends with a one-minute mission to try together away from the screen.

**Try it:** https://6h30.github.io/little-play-english/

This is an early trial version. It has not been tested with children yet. No ads, no accounts, no tracking: progress is saved only in the browser on your device.

## What is inside

- 5 levels (ages 1–2, 2–3, 3–4, 4–5, 5+), 26 themes, about 220 words, sentences and letters.
- Each word is tracked in 5 steps; words you have met come back after a while.
- A parent area (hold the gear button for 2 seconds, then type a number written in words) with progress and real-life missions, in English and Vietnamese.
- Works offline after the first visit. Add it to the home screen to use it like an app.
